I
Saturday · 31 October

Sacred Geometry

10:00–14:30 · Ruska 12, Prague

Sacred Geometry is the pattern behind all life. In a hands-on half day with Diana & Radu Coman, you learn to work with it to lift your own energy and raise your frequency.

It is not about one symbol. It is about life itself: the foundation of how everything came into existence, and how you can use these energies for yourself.

In the class you will

  • Recognize the basic geometric forms and the quality each one carries
  • Learn to work with them deliberately, not just admire them
  • Clear, protect and consecrate a space: your home, your practice, or yourself
  • Use geometry for focus, meditation, manifestation and healing
  • Start to see the hidden order in everyday life
  • Just 7 spots
  • In English
  • Short breaks & tea
Book Sacred Geometry · Sat 31 Oct

If you have any questions, reach out to us on WhatsApp.

II
Sunday · 1 November

Astral Travel

10:00–14:30 · Ruska 12, Prague

A guided class in the same studio. We practice the art of Astral Travel, the conscious projection of your awareness beyond the physical body.

In the Sacred Geometry class you recognize and connect with the inner fabric of the universe. In Astral Travel you walk through the door and start exploring it.

In the class you will

  • Understand the structure of the subtle planes
  • Practice leaving and re-entering the body, with protection
  • Move with intention and meet your guides
  • Return remembering your experience and the insights you received
  • Just 7 spots
  • In English
  • Short breaks & tea
Book Astral Travel · Sun 1 Nov

If you have any questions, reach out to us on WhatsApp.
